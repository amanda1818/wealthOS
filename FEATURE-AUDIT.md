# FEATURE-AUDIT.md — Honest Feature Audit

*Written 2026-08-02, at the close of a long build session, for the person opening this repo again in September. Audited against PRODUCT-BRIEF.md and REVAMP.md. This is deliberately skeptical, not reassuring — every "WORKING" claim below states how it was verified.*

## Methodology and its limits (read this before the table)

- I have live browser access to **one already-authenticated session** for your real household (Amanda + Bisma), via `localhost:3000` against the real Supabase project. Everything marked "verified live" was actually clicked, not just read.
- I **cannot sign out and back in** — I can't type a password. That means I could not test: sign-out → sign-in round trip, the actual Google OAuth flow to completion, password reset delivery, or true two-simultaneous-user behavior (seeing your data change live in another partner's session). Anywhere this matters, it's called out explicitly as NOT VERIFIED, not guessed as working.
- Your real household currently has **near-zero real financial data entered** (both partners show `monthlyIncome: 0` in Settings, and most weeks have no new transactions). Several brand-new features are code-complete but will only ever show empty/quiet states until real numbers are entered — that's flagged per-feature below, not hidden.
- **Disclosure:** while testing the AI command bar during this audit, one test message ("Coffee 50000") actually posted a real Rp 50,000 transaction to your live household data. I reverted it immediately using the app's own Undo button and confirmed net worth returned to its prior value. Nothing else was modified.

## Top 5 to fix/build first in September

1. **Root-cause the `ai-gateway` 502s.** This is the single biggest finding of this audit — the AI backend that powers Chat, the Whisper command bar, receipt OCR, and AI priority generation is confirmed intermittently failing server-side, not just occasionally slow. Check the Supabase Edge Function logs first; this affects more of the app's "smart" surface than anything else on this list.
2. **Fix the hardcoded "Greetings David & Victoria" seed message.** Small fix, real impact: every brand-new household that ever signs up gets this wrong-name greeting permanently, not just your test data. Either remove the seed message or make it name-agnostic.
3. **Build real password reset.** Confirmed genuinely missing, not just broken. Real users will get locked out with no recovery path — this was already flagged pre-launch-blocking in PICKUP-NOTE.md and PRODUCT-BRIEF.md.
4. **Resolve Google auth one way or the other.** Either fix the Supabase provider config or remove the button — right now it's an unverified dangling promise either way.
5. **Decide Together's remaining orphaned components.** MergerSimulator/SettlementCard/ValuesCompass/VampireHunter are dead code sitting unmounted since before this session. Either commit to building them for real (ValuesCompass needs actual scoring logic, not just wiring) or formally cut them per REVAMP.md's "don't keep dead code just in case" rule — right now they're neither.

*Everything else — the 5-surface restructure, Freedom engine, Together Layers 1 & 2, the Weekly Report, the NaN fix — is real, tested, and working as designed. The gaps above are specific and fixable, not systemic.*
